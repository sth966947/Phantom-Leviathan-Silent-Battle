        How to play
        • Stay slow to stay hidden. Above about 10 knots you get loud and your sonar goes deaf. The towed array hears farther and covers astern at 12 knots or less, once you've held a course for 90 seconds.
        • Identify by signature. Select a contact to see its machinery and propeller lines. The crew suggests classes with a match %. More lines appear as the contact gets louder or closer. Accept a match to classify it.
        • Look. At periscope depth you get an exact fix on ships within 8 miles, but they may spot your periscope. Click the ship (or press V) to see it through the periscope and compare it with the identification book.
        • Radar. At periscope depth, press R. Ships inside 15 miles are fixed and identified on the first sweep; out to 25 miles you see them but can't tell what they are. Helicopters and planes down on the water show inside 15 miles, aircraft flying high out to 100. In 1941 nobody can hear it. In 2029 the Il-38's receiver can, and it will come looking. In 1941, radar is the only way to find aircraft.
        • Listen for aircraft. In 2029, sonobuoy splashes and a Ka-27's dipping sonar are on your sonar, and give away where the aircraft are. JATM and SM-6 are fire and forget: they fly down the selected air contact's bearing (or your heading) and take the first aircraft their seeker sees within 50 miles and 45° either side.
        • Match. With a contact selected, Course steers its course, Bearing steers straight at it, and Speed (Z) keeps pace with it. They keep following until you click them off, steer or order a speed yourself, or select another contact. Course and Bearing can't both be on.
        • Build a solution. Range firms up the longer you track, and each new leg (a turn of 40° or more) improves it sharply.
        • Paused? Space pauses and shows the briefing with a live checklist of the mission's objectives.
        • Escorts. Destroyers and corvettes come hunting after a torpedo hits or a periscope is seen. Their depth charges are set for 50 to 400 feet: go to 700 feet and slow down.
        • Shoot. Mk 48s are wire-guided to your solution. NSM and Tomahawk fly to your solution and home on the ship nearest it, friend or foe, so a poor solution can hit the wrong ship. ASROC drops a Mk 54 on your solution 1 to 15 miles out. JATM and SM-6 shoot down aircraft. Missiles launch from 150 feet or shallower.
