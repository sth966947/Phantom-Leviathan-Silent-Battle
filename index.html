      Training mission and duel

      Sink the Bismarck

      Denmark Strait · 24 May 1941 · 0300

      A shakedown cruise ends in a time vortex. Tang surfaces in May 1941 with no GPS and only Morse on the radio. Bismarck and Prinz Eugen are breaking out into the Atlantic at 27 knots, with the British cruisers Suffolk and Norfolk shadowing them by radar. Sink Bismarck before she gets past you. Don't hit the British.

      Duel: Kazan

      Bear Gap · March 2029 · 0200

      Back in the present war. A Yasen-M, Kazan, is in your patrol box, quiet and armed. Find it on passive sonar and sink it before it finds you.

      • Stay slow to stay hidden. Above about 10 knots you get loud and your sonar goes deaf. The towed array hears farther and covers astern at 12 knots or less, once you've held a course for 90 seconds.
      • Identify by signature. Select a contact to see its machinery and propeller lines. The crew suggests classes with a match %. More lines appear as the contact gets louder or closer. Accept a match to classify it.
      • Look. At periscope depth you get exact bearing, range and class on ships within 8 miles, but they may spot your periscope.
      • Build a solution. Range firms up the longer you track, and each new leg (a turn of 40° or more) improves it sharply.
      • Shoot. Mk 48s are wire-guided to your solution. Harpoon and Tomahawk fly to your solution and home on the ship nearest it, friend or foe, so a poor solution can hit the wrong ship. Missiles launch from 150 feet or shallower.
